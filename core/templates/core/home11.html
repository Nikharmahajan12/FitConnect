MEET YOUR TRAINER

Nitish Mahajan.

Your goals. Your journey. Your stronger self.

I'm Nitish Mahajan, a passionate Personal Trainer with 5 years of experience in exercise and fitness, dedicated to helping people become stronger, healthier, and more confident versions of themselves.

My approach goes beyond traditional workouts. I create personalized training programmes based on your individual goals, fitness level, lifestyle, and needs.

Whether your goal is weight loss, muscle building, strength development, improving mobility, or returning to an active lifestyle, I'm here to guide you with purpose and consistency.

I also offer rehabilitation training programmes designed to support improvements in movement, mobility, strength, and overall physical function through carefully planned exercises.
